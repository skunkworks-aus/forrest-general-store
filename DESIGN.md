# Design direction

The page leads with the most characteristic thing about the place: arriving at a small white shopfront in the Otway forest.

## Colours

| Name | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#0b1f1b` | Top bar, footer, page ground outside |
| Pine | `#14332f` | Text on light surfaces, main buttons |
| Paper | `#f4f7f1` | Cards and the Visit panel |
| Ochre | `#e3a72f` | Accents, focus ring, "coming soon" tags |
| Timber | `#2b1b12` and `#7a4526` | Inside-the-store ground and board frames |
| Slate | `#1f2325` | Chalkboard panels inside |

## Type

Barlow Condensed 700 for headings, Barlow 400 to 600 for text. Body text 17px, headings 28 to 84px.

## Shape

Square corners and no shadows. Panels are flat, like painted signs and chalkboards.

## Motion

The drawn journey moves only when the visitor scrolls. Steam and drifting cloud are the one ambient touch. With reduced motion set, ambient movement stops and the scene steps with the scroll.

## Reasons for choices that could look like defaults

- **Dark ground with an ochre accent:** the forest road is dark green and the store's interior is timber with brass lights; ochre is the brass.
- **Four equal boards inside:** they stand for four real areas of the shop (front counter, pie cabinet, shelves, post office counter).
